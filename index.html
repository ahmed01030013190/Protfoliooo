Key Insights

  • Over half of orders (57.78%) receive 5-star reviews, alongside a healthy average review score of 4.09 — indicating generally strong customer satisfaction.
  • Revenue is concentrated among a small group of VIP customers, with the top 10 customers by revenue ranging from roughly $4.6K to $13.4K each.
  • Net contribution by region is fairly balanced across Mansoura (34.6%), Assiut (33.08%) and Suez (32.32%), suggesting no single region dominates profitability.
  • Cash on Delivery and Prepaid are the two leading payment/status contributors to net contribution, with Wallet trailing behind.

Business Recommendations

  • Build a retention program around top VIP customers, since a concentrated group drives a disproportionate share of revenue.
  • Investigate operational costs relative to delivery fees, given contribution margin sits at 13.2% against 266.32K EGP in operational cost.
  • Monitor SLA and delivery metrics highlighted in the Logistics suite to protect the strong customer satisfaction levels already achieved.
  • Compare regional performance drivers between Mansoura, Assiut and Suez to replicate what is working in the leading region.
